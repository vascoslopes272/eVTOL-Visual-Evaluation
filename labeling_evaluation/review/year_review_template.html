A year is an upper bound of when the aircraft first existed publicly. “Existed by” means a prototype existed by then, even if sub-scale. a video or article dated Y only proves it existed by Y. Pick what kind of date it is; confirm only if the sentence really dates this aircraft.
definitions of the kinds of date (click)
design / programme start: development began (“started designing in 2017”).
announced / unveiled: the aircraft was presented as a project in order to start being developed. Showing an already built aircraft is not this; that is “existed by”.
first flight / flight tests: it flew (never a concept design).
production / entry into service: series production or operation began.
publication date only: the date of an article or video about the aircraft; says nothing about when anything happened.
existed by this year: a prototype existed by then, even if sub-scale (never a concept design).
NOT this aircraft: the sentence is about the team, the company or another model; the year is discarded.
