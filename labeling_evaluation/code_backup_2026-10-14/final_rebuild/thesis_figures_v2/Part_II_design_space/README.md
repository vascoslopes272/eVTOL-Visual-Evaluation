# Part II (Design space) figures, v2

Relabelled, simplified and regrouped versions of a subset of the Q6 / Q7 thesis figures (`viz/thesis_v2_part2.py`). No number or computation is changed from `thesis_figures/`, a sibling folder this one never writes into; every builder reads its frame from `viz/thesis_q6.py` or `thesis_q7.py` unchanged and only re-draws, relabels or regroups it. Generated by `notebooks/50_thesis_figures_v2_part2.ipynb` (notebook 49 was already taken by the LLM-input review). Each figure is written as .pdf + .png + .csv + .json; each table as .csv + .json, as in Part I.

## Terminology

- **configuration** (never archetype); **design** (never *aircraft* as a count; "designs" on axes); **assignee** (never filer); **cohort** (never window); **architecture** (never class / architecture class)
- **Multicopter** (never Multirotor; the data code stays MR); **Tilt Frame TF** (never Tilt Body TB); **Tilt Propulsor TP**; **SLC** is the code of Lift + Cruise
- levels of description: **C**, **C·W·T**, **C·U** (C = architecture; C·W·T = architecture · number of wings · whether any propulsor tilts; C·U = architecture · propulsor-unit band 0–3 / 4 / 5–6 / 7–8 / 9+)
- a C·U configuration is printed as architecture code + propulsor-unit band, e.g. `SLC 9+`, `TP 0–3`, `TF 4`

## Style

Part I v2 style; 6.3 in wide; smallest text 8 pt at print size; no in-plot text boxes; no label overlapping a mark or another label (checked on the rendered figures); a legend only for what is drawn; at most six colours, the rest grey.

## Why the rarefaction size is 40 (and the 300 draws)

**40 is a default with no stated reason.** It first appears as `n: int = 40` in `hill_numbers` / `hill_by_window` of `archive/.../dataset_facts/la_tables.py` (already there in the 2026-09-22 backups), was carried into `dominant_design` / `dd_balance` and then into `analysis.rarefy_n` of `labelling_analysis.yaml`, whose comment ("a good number … a reasonable balance between statistical power and computational efficiency") gives no cohort-size rule. The cohorts it rarefies are <= 2011 63, 2012-15 58, 2016-19 211, 2020-23 237, 2024-26 (partial) 56 designs at C (C·W·T: 63, 58, 211, 236, 55); 40 is under all of them, the smallest being 2024–26 (partial, 55 at C·W·T, which is not read) and 2012–15 (58) among the complete cohorts. The 300 is likewise a hard-coded `obs_draws=300` of `lib/dominant_design.balance_band` (the permutation draws are `perm_draws=40`, permutations 200); no rule fixed it either.

If `rarefy_n` were set to the smallest cohort read (58, the 2012–15 cohort), every ²D in the figures and the verdicts that use it (condition 2) would be recomputed on larger samples, so their values would change a little, and the cohorts of 2012–15 would be read on all their designs. Nothing was changed.

## Judgement calls

- **Notebook number**: the request said 49; `49_review_llm_inputs.ipynb` already exists, so this is notebook **50** (`build_50.py`, `write_readme_50.py`). Rename if 49 should be this one.
- **Output folder**: `thesis_figures_v2/Part_II_design_space/`, beside `Part_I_evidence_base/` (read from "Figures/").
- **File names** that the request did not rename keep the originals' words (`q6_propulsor_units_per_class`, `q6_arrangement_by_class`); the words *class* and *archetype* appear only in those file names, never in a label.
- **Discovery curve, random-order band**: the frame holds the random-order expectation as ONE curve (analytic rarefaction, no spread), so a band is not available without a new computation. It is drawn as a thin dashed grey line, no fill; a true band would need the rarefaction variance. The "spread inside a year" band is dropped, as asked.
- **Discovery curve, per-panel y axis**: the three levels end at 12, 37 and 42 configurations, so each panel has its own y scale (the curve covers the designs of complete years, 37 at C·W·T, 42 at C·U, against 39 and 43 in the all-years table). The first design of 2016 and of 2020 are design numbers 122 and 333 at every level.
- **Core figure**: markers are one size and one shape (the original's bubble area = designs and triangle = first seen 2016 or later are dropped, since the legend is limited to architectures). The 3 labelled non-core configurations are the three with the largest 2016–23 share outside the core (TP 9+, CVT 4, SLC 0–3). The median guide lines are dropped; the rim is the configurations at or under the median share.
- **Core figure, 2024–26**: not added. The y axis (assignees per design) is computed on 2016–23 only, so a hollow 2024–26 marker would need either a new y or a different y on the same row; the 54 designs of 2024–26 also spread over 33 configurations (1–5 each). Left out.
- **One name for the lowest propulsor-unit band: 0–3**, in every table and figure label (Table 6, the levels table, the core figure's point labels, the ducting ticks). The data's own bin is 0–3 (`frames.ROTOR_BINS`); `frames.UNIT_BANDS` prints 1–3 for display, which is why the figures and the levels table said 1–3. One design is recorded with 0 units and sits in this band.
- **`q6_half_of_cohort`**: the denominator is the cohort's designs in the 33 C·U configurations of five or more designs (as the original heatmap); ranks 1–20 of 33 are drawn. All four complete cohorts are drawn, and 2024–26 (dashed, faded, hollow marker, labelled incomplete) (≤2011 and 2012–15 hold 61 and 57 designs there). The ≤2011 and 2012–15 lines cross 50 % at the same rank (5), so their two markers sit on top of each other.
- **`q6_propulsor_units_per_class`**: HB and PFV have no propulsor record and are left out and named once under the axis; x labels count the designs drawn.
- **II1 tables and appendix**: the heatmap's HB and PFV columns stay (hatched = no record), as in the original.
- **Fonts**: `plots.feature_heat` and `plots.duct_bands` printed 7.5 pt text, so the appendix figures use local copies at 8 pt (`_feature_heat`, `_duct_bands`), identical otherwise; the white boxes behind the duct diamond labels are removed.
